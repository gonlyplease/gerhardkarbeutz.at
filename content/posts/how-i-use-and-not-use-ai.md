## Intro

I am a 26 year student that is currently starting a career in cyber security. My journey in tech started in high school where I was pursuing a mechatronics major. Then I continued with a business degree. While studying I start co-developing a mobile app and getting my first insights in software engineering besides microcontrollers. In 2021 ChatGPT launched and I started using it and doing my experiments on its abilities. Since then I went through many phases of using it way too much to not using it at all. I explored many of the options out there with all the varying models and harnesses that came along. Now with my collected insights, fails and struggles its time to write a future proof guide for myself on how to use and **even more important** how to not use AI.

Especially this week has probably been the most nerve wrecking phase I recognised personally since the hype started growing. In a recent HackerNews complaint about too many AI news someone linked a HN AI free feed. [1]



Where I wont use AI:

Writing in emails, research, proposal, notes, reports etc
